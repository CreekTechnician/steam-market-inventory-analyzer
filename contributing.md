**🎒 Steam Market Inventory Analyzer** is the complete English-language guide to building a Steam inventory analyzer. Learn how to fetch inventory data from the Steam API, price items via the market API, compute portfolio value, track price history, and export results — with runnable Python and a clean dashboard. Built for traders, data analysts, and Steam users who want real inventory insight. **Completely free.** No signup. No paywall. No hidden chapters.
